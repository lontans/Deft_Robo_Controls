## Current state of the repo (bench truth)

### What is proven

- **Left YAM arm (CH1):** discover → progressive MIT green → CLEAR cruise under continuous.
- **Base (bench map):** CH5 `0x70`, CH6 `0x75`, CH6 Damiao `0x06` spin under continuous when healthy; daisy reset/enable ordering documented.
- **TX bandwidth:** plant hold matrix healthy at 40/100/200 Hz (Mission Impossible M1).
- **PDU soft-kill / V/I (UART sim):** handshake + undervoltage + overcurrent parks (M2), once PDU is live (not COMMS_LOSS).
- **Multi-joint CLEAR (M3)** and **faster base + DXL continuous (M4)** passed in-suite (operator: M4 quiet; M1/M2 holds were vibratory by design).
- **Soft-kill park** from continuous (dashboard follow-mode flag or host hooks).

### Open / flaky (2026-07-25)

Documented in `docs/peripherals/continuous-ops.md` (“Open bench issues”):

1. **CH5 RobStride `0x74`** — silent on CAN (`found=0`, no discover) even though it answered earlier the same day; `0x70` still OK. Treat as HW/ID/daisy second-node until probe returns.
2. **DXL neck** — present reads work; torque-on goals did not move FB in the last session (regressed vs 2026-07-24 tracking).
3. **J2 brace** — when lifting J2 in CLEAR, other joints sometimes do not hold pose hard enough.

Also still product-shaped work, not “first light”:
